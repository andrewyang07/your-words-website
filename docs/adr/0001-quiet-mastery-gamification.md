# Quiet Mastery Gamification

> Status: Superseded. Deep Memorization does not retain mastery, scores, streaks, schedules, or completion counts. See `docs/prd-daily-review.md`.

We will make Daily Review feel game-like through visible mastery, review progress, streaks, and verse-level growth rather than social competition. The site may keep rankings as a discovery surface, but rankings should not become the primary motivation loop because Bible memorization should stay personal, reflective, and focused on durable recall.
